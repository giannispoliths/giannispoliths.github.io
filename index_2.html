Cross-border electricity flows

Team project, Python, pandas, Matplotlib, Tkinter

A desktop application for exploring Greece's electricity imports and exports with Albania, Bulgaria, Italy, North Macedonia and Turkey over 2022–2023. It processes the raw data into daily, monthly and yearly views, with charts, hourly profiles, peak-exchange statistics and a comparison against Germany, France and Denmark.
